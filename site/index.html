about this collection

Time coverage

The main window covers papers first released from 2 Aug 2023 to 2 Aug 2026. Earlier work is included only when it is needed to understand how DiT methods evolved.

Inclusion criteria

The collection favors papers that introduce a reusable architectural, training, systems, or evaluation idea. Each paper is also labeled by how directly it relates to DiT.

Sources & maintenance

Metadata is checked against conference pages and arXiv. PDF links point to the original source; the structured catalog and research notes are maintained on GitHub.
