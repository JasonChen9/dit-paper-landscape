about this collection

Scope

Research on diffusion and flow models, efficient systems, reinforcement learning, world models, robotics and agent workflows, alongside foundational references. All dates are included by default; the timeline compresses earlier work while keeping it selectable.

Classification & browsing

Each paper has one primary topic based on its main contribution. Application domains, system layers and collection sources provide independent filters. The map is arranged by primary topic, with links showing topic-tag similarity as reading suggestions.

Content & sources

Bilingual cards summarize the background, challenge, method and reported results. Expand Abstract for the original English text or a full AI-translated Chinese version with its source. Verified conference and journal pages take priority; preprint links and date precision are retained.
